<b>Vibe</b> is a local music player focused on fluidity, simplicity, and minimalist design. Built for those who prefer to carry their own audio files on the device without hassle, ads, or data collection.<br /><br /><b>Key features:</b><br /><br />• <b>ExoPlayer Native Engine:</b> High-fidelity, battery-efficient playback powered by Android's robust multimedia core.<br />• <b>100% Offline and ad-free:</b> No internet connection required for any function.<br />• <b>Playlist management:</b> Create, edit, and organize your playlists easily.<br />• <b>Local backup:</b> Export and import your playlists in JSON format (to clipboard) so you never lose your lists when reinstalling.<br />• <b>Dedicated navigation:</b> Individual views for albums and artists, plus a dedicated search section to find what you want instantly.<br />• <b>Lightweight and efficient:</b> Optimized to smoothly handle libraries with thousands of songs.<br /><br /><b>Open Source:</b> Vibe is free and open-source software licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.<br /><b>Languages:</b> The interface is available in English and Spanish, auto-detected from your system or selectable in Settings.<br /><br /><b>Minimum requirements for a smooth experience:</b><br /><br />• <b>Android 10 (API level 29)</b> or higher.<br />• <b>System WebView (85 >=)</b>.<br /><br />Note: this does not mean older versions are incompatible — Android 10 with WebView 85 is simply the oldest environment we were able to test Vibe on.
