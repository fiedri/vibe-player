<b>Vibe</b> is a local music player focused on fluidity, simplicity, and minimalist design. Built for those who prefer to carry their own audio files on the device without hassle, ads, or data collection.

<b>Key features:</b>

• <b>100% Offline and ad-free:</b> No internet connection required for any function.
• <b>Playlist management:</b> Create, edit, and organize your playlists easily.
• <b>Local backup:</b> Export and import your playlists in JSON format (to clipboard) so you never lose your lists when reinstalling.
• <b>Dedicated navigation:</b> Individual views for albums and artists, plus a dedicated search section to find what you want instantly.
• <b>Lightweight and efficient:</b> Designed with virtualized rendering to smoothly handle libraries with thousands of songs.

<b>Open Source:</b> Vibe is free and open-source software licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later.
<b>Note: The app user interface is currently available only in Spanish.</b>
