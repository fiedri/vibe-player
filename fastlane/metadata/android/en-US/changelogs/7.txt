Public Beta Launch (v0.5.2)

Vibe is a minimalist, 100% offline local music player.

Features in this version:
- Smooth local playback.
- Views organized by Songs, Albums, Artists, and Playlists.
- Complete playlist management: creation, editing, and duplicate filtering.
- Backup and restoration of playlists via JSON.
- Smart playback queue, multi-selection, and sorting.
- Dedicated search and song favorites manager.

Tested and verified on Android 10, 13, and 14.
